[center][size=6][color=#FF4D6D][b]♥ Resource Respawn ♥[/b][/color][/size][/center]

[center][color=#FF4D6D][b]Mined a spot dry? Don't cry — give it a little love and it comes back. ♥[/b][/color][/center]

[center][color=#FF4D6D][b]★ v2.0.0 — mined deposits now respawn LIVE. No Save. No reload. No swimming away. Break a node, wait your timer, and it grows back in front of you. ★[/b][/color][/center]

In Subnautica 2, loose pickups and wildlife slowly respawn on their own, but mined ore nodes are finite — the game wants you to farm those with a Metal Farm. [b]Resource Respawn[/b] gives every gathered resource a second life on a cooldown [b]you[/b] control, so your favourite spots never truly run dry. ♥

Everything is configurable [b]in-game[/b] — no file editing needed — if you have Mod Settings installed.

[size=4][color=#FF4D6D][b]♥ What's new in 2.0.0[/b][/color][/size]
In 1.0.0, the medium and large nodes you break with a Sonic Resonator only came back after a Save + reload + moving far away. That was the mod's biggest limitation, and it's gone.

It turns out the game [i]destroys[/i] a deposit the moment you mine it — so there was never anything left to "un-gather", and nothing in the game's world-population system offers a way to bring one back. So 2.0.0 stops trying to revive the old node and instead [b]builds a brand new one on the same spot[/b], at the same angle, once your timer runs out.

[list]
[*][b]Everything respawns live now[/b] — deposits, loose pickups, water slugs. Same spot, same rotation, no reload.[/*]
[*][b]It really is the node[/b] — the new one is a full resource node: mine it, get the resource, mine it again. Farm one spot as long as you like.[/*]
[*][b]Much lighter on your framerate[/b] — 1.0.0 reflected over every resource actor in the world on every scan, which could stutter every 5 seconds. 2.0.0 only looks at what is actually near you: roughly 100x less work per scan.[/*]
[*][b]No more crash[/b] when swimming between areas.[/*]
[/list]

[size=4][color=#FF4D6D][b]♥ How it works[/b][/color][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Mined deposits[/b] — the game destroys the node when you break it, so the mod remembers what stood there (which node, where, at what angle) and spawns a fresh one on your timer.[/*]
[*][b]Loose pickups & water slugs[/b] — these survive being harvested, so the mod simply clears their "gathered" state, which is cheaper.[/*]
[*][b]Non-destructive[/b] — no save editing. Uninstall and the world is vanilla again after the next reload. ♥[/*]
[/list]

[size=4][color=#FF4D6D][b]♥ Compatibility[/b][/color][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Existing saves[/b] — safe to add mid-playthrough. Resources you harvested before installing come back too: the mod notices the spot is empty and refills it. No new game needed. (Thanks to Fe1eNinamu24 for confirming.)[/*]
[*][b]Game version[/b] — reported working on Subnautica 2 [b]1.1[/b]. (Thanks to Fe1eNinamu24.)[/*]
[*][b]Multiplayer / Co-op[/b] — see Known Issues. Host and client must both install the mod with the [b]same settings[/b]; it does not sync between players.[/*]
[/list]

[size=4][color=#FF4D6D][b]♥ Configuration[/b][/color][/size]
With Mod Settings installed, open [b]Settings → Mods → Resource Respawn[/b] in-game. Toggle each resource and drag the respawn-time slider; changes apply within a few seconds. Without Mod Settings, edit [i]ResourceRespawn/Scripts/config.lua[/i].

[size=4][color=#FF4D6D][b]♥ Known Issues[/b][/color][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Stay near the spot.[/b] The mod only acts within about 150m of you — that range check is what keeps it light on your framerate. Mine a node and swim off to another biome and it will simply wait: it comes back once you are in range again and the timer has passed.[/*]
[*][b]Respawned nodes are not written into your save[/b] — they are spawned at runtime. In practice you will not notice: on load the mod sees the spot is empty and refills it on your timer anyway.[/*]
[*][b]Multiplayer / Co-op[/b] — the 2.0.0 spawning is local to each machine and does not replicate, so a respawned deposit may not appear for your friends. Everyone needs the mod with the same settings. Co-op was tested on 1.0.0 for the small nodes (thanks Fe1eNinamu24), but the new deposit spawning is [b]not yet verified in co-op[/b] — reports very welcome. ♥[/*]
[*][b]Troilite[/b] is shown in the menu as [i](unsupported)[/i]. Its Mineralized Clinker is a static object with no gathered state the mod can read, so toggling it does nothing — it is listed only for awareness.[/*]
[/list]
