[center][size=6][color=#FF4D6D][b]♥ Resource Respawn ♥[/b][/color][/size][/center]

[center][color=#FF4D6D][b]Mined a spot dry? Don't cry — give it a little love and it comes back. ♥[/b][/color][/center]

[center][color=#FF4D6D][b]★ v2.1: Troilite finally respawns, and the spots you emptied last session are waiting for you right after login. ★[/b][/color][/center]

In Subnautica 2, loose pickups and wildlife slowly respawn on their own, but mined ore nodes are finite — the game wants you to farm those with a Metal Farm. [b]Resource Respawn[/b] gives every gathered resource a second life on a cooldown [b]you[/b] control, so your favourite spots never truly run dry. ♥

Everything is configurable [b]in-game[/b] — no file editing needed — if you have Mod Settings installed.

[size=4][color=#FF4D6D][b]♥ What's new in 2.1[/b][/color][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Troilite respawns now.[/b] The one resource marked [i](unsupported)[/i] is supported. Its Mineralized Clinker turned out to be a real resource node under the hood, so it comes back like everything else. Toggle it in Mod Settings.[/*]
[*][b]Refill on load.[/b] Spots you emptied in an earlier session, or before you even installed the mod, come back about 15 seconds after you get near them. No more waiting the full timer on every login. On by default; the toggle sits right under the timer slider. (Thanks Fe1eNinamu24 for the idea!)[/*]
[*][b]Respawned nodes keep their original size.[/b] They used to come back shrunken.[/*]
[*][b]The respawn slider is easier to tune:[/b] 5s to 10min (was 60min), default 120s. Its description now also explains why very low timers can cost a little performance.[/*]
[/list]

And everything from 2.0 still holds: deposits, loose pickups and water slugs all respawn [b]live[/b], right where they stood. No Save, no reload, no swimming away. The new node is a full resource node: mine it, get the resource, mine it again. ♥

[size=4][color=#FF4D6D][b]♥ How it works[/b][/color][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Mined deposits[/b] — the game destroys the node when you break it, so the mod remembers what stood there (which node, where, at what angle) and spawns a fresh one on your timer.[/*]
[*][b]Loose pickups & water slugs[/b] — these survive being harvested, so the mod simply clears their "gathered" state, which is cheaper.[/*]
[*][b]Non-destructive[/b] — no save editing. Uninstall and the world is vanilla again after the next reload. ♥[/*]
[/list]

[size=4][color=#FF4D6D][b]♥ Features[/b][/color][/size]
[list]
[*]Every gathered resource respawns after a cooldown you choose[/*]
[*]One global respawn-time slider (5s–10min, default 120s)[/*]
[*]Refill on load: spots emptied in an earlier session refill about 15s after you get near them[/*]
[*]Per-resource on/off toggles: Titanium, Copper, Quartz, Salt, Water Slug, Lead, Silver, Sulfur, Gold, Lithium, Atacamite, Celestine, Conduit Crystal, Creature Enamel, Axum Bacterial Culture and Troilite (both normally finite — this mod lets them respawn)[/*]
[*]Auto-detected language (English included; built so other languages are easy to add)[/*]
[/list]

[size=4][color=#FF4D6D][b]♥ Compatibility[/b][/color][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Existing saves[/b] — safe to add mid-playthrough. Resources you harvested before installing come back too, about 15s after you get near them (Refill on load). No new game needed. (Thanks to Fe1eNinamu24 for confirming.)[/*]
[*][b]Game version[/b] — reported working on 1.1. (Thanks to Fe1eNinamu24.)[/*]
[*][b]Multiplayer / Co-op[/b] — see Known Issues. Host and client must both install the mod with the [b]same settings[/b]; it does not sync between players.[/*]
[/list]

[size=4][color=#FF4D6D][b]♥ Configuration[/b][/color][/size]
With Mod Settings installed, open [b]Settings → Mods → Resource Respawn[/b] in-game. Toggle each resource and drag the respawn-time slider; changes apply within a few seconds. Without Mod Settings, edit [i]ResourceRespawn/Scripts/config.lua[/i].

[size=4][color=#FF4D6D][b]♥ Known Issues[/b][/color][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Stay near the spot.[/b] The mod only acts within about 150m of you — that range check is what keeps it light on your framerate. Mine a node and swim off to another biome and it will simply wait: it comes back once you are in range again and the timer has passed.[/*]
[*][b]Respawned nodes are not written into your save[/b] — they are spawned at runtime. In practice you will not notice: with Refill on load they are back about 15s after you get near the spot.[/*]
[*][b]Multiplayer / Co-op[/b] — the spawning is local to each machine and does not replicate, so a respawned deposit may not appear for your friends. Everyone needs the mod with the same settings. Co-op was tested on 1.0 for the small nodes (thanks Fe1eNinamu24), but deposit spawning is [b]not yet verified in co-op[/b] — reports very welcome. ♥[/*]
[*][b]Yield is the game's roll.[/b] A respawned node rolls its own piece count, so it can give more or fewer pieces than the node it replaced.[/*]
[/list]
